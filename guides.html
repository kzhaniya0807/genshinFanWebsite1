FAQ

What should beginners focus on first?

Beginners can start by exploring Teyvat, completing quests, leveling characters and learning how elemental reactions work.

How should I build a character?

Start with the character level and weapon, then upgrade talents and choose suitable artifacts.

What is Original Resin used for?

Original Resin is used to claim rewards from activities such as Domains, Ley Lines and certain bosses.
