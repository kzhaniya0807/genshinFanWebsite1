What is Genshin Impact?

Genshin Impact is an open-world action RPG by HoYoverse. Play as the Traveler, journey through the seven nations of Teyvat, master the elements, and uncover the secrets of the gods.

Quick Facts

  • 7 nations to explore
  • 70+ playable characters
  • PC, Mobile & PlayStation
  • Free to play

FAQ

Is Genshin Impact free to play?

Yes. The base game is free; optional in-game purchases exist.

Which platforms are supported?

PC, iOS, Android, and PlayStation 4 / 5.

Do I need to play the story in order?

Yes — regions unlock in order, so following the Archon Quest is recommended.
