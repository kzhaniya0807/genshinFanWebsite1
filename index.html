What is Genshin Impact?

Genshin Impact is an open-world action RPG by HoYoverse. Play as the Traveler, journey through the seven nations of Teyvat, master the elements, and uncover the secrets of the gods.

Quick Facts

  • 7 nations to explore
  • 70+ playable characters
  • PC, Mobile & PlayStation
  • Free to play

Game Features

Elemental Combat

Combine Pyro, Hydro, Electro and more to trigger reactions.

Open World

Climb mountains, glide across valleys, swim through oceans.

Story Quests

Follow deep storylines for every character you meet.

Co-op Mode

Team up with friends to defeat bosses together.

Beautiful Art

Enjoy a stunning anime-inspired world.

Regular Updates

New regions and characters every version.
