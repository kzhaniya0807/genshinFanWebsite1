Elemental Synergy Tips

Vaporize Reaction

Combines Pyro and Hydro for multiplied damage output.

Melt Reaction

Combines Pyro and Cryo for critical damage amplifiers.

Aggravate Reaction

Combines Dendro and Electro for increased elemental damage.

Hyperbloom Reaction

Combines Dendro and Hydro to create Bloom cores, then triggers Electro to fire homing Sprawling Shots.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How do I get 5-Star characters in Genshin Impact?

5-Star characters are obtained through the Wish system (gacha) using Intertwined or Acquaint Fates.

What is Elemental Resonance?

When you have specific combinations of elemental character types in your party, your whole team receives bonuses.
