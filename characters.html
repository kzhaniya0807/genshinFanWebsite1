    FAQ

    How do I get 5-Star characters in Genshin Impact?

    5-Star characters are obtained through tje Wish system (gacha) using Intertwined or Acquaint Fates.

    What is Elemental Resonance?

    When you have specific combinations of elemental character types in your party, your whole team receives bonuses.

    How do Elemental Reactions work in combat?

    Elemental Reactions occur when you combine two or more elements on a single target, dealing extra damage or applying status effects like Freeze or Vaporize.
